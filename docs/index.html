How this works

An AI agent gets $50 of fake money and trades real Polymarket markets that resolve within a week. Prices, order books and taker fees are live. Only the money is fake.

  • Every 6 hours it reads the busiest markets, searches the web, estimates odds and decides whether to bet.
  • Same rules as the viral reel: at most 6% of the bankroll per bet, sized with Kelly.
  • Its real API bill is deducted from the bankroll. It has to pay for itself.
  • Every 20 minutes a cloud job re-prices open bets and settles finished markets.
  • "Random picks" makes coin-flip bets on the same markets with the same sizing and fees. If the AI can't beat it, it has no edge.
